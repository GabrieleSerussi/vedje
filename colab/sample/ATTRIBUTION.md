# Sample frames

`frame_00.jpg` to `frame_15.jpg` are 16 frames sampled uniformly from "Peacock walking and eating" by Mx. Granger, Wikimedia Commons, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peacock_walking_and_eating.webm, released under the CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The frames are resized to 512 by 288 pixels.
